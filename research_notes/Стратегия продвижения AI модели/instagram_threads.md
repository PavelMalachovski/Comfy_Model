# Instagram and Threads growth for a photoreal AI persona, and routing that traffic to Fanvue (2025–2026)

Research date: 2026-11-21. English-language sources only. Source labels: **[Meta]** = official Meta/Instagram/Threads page or Oversight Board; **[Press]** = journalism; **[Data]** = analytics-company or academic study; **[Vendor]** = blog by a company selling tools or services to adult creators or OFM agencies (commercial interest, unaudited); **[Anecdotal]** = practitioner course, forum or Telegram claim. Anything dated before 2025 is flagged **(pre-2025, possibly outdated)**. Several Meta Transparency Center and Help Center pages would not render for my fetch tool. ## 1. Meta policies that matter for an AI persona (AI labels, authenticity, sexual solicitation, recommendations, Account Status)

### Takeaway
Meta has no rule against a fictional AI persona as such. Three rule sets actually bind: (a) mandatory self-disclosure for photorealistic AI **video and audio** (still images get labelled automatically, but only if C2PA/IPTC signals are detected); (b) the Adult Sexual Solicitation policy, under which an implicit offer plus a suggestive element means removal, and adult-subscription links are restricted to viewers 18+; (c) the Recommendation Guidelines, which keep sexually suggestive content visible to followers but out of Explore, Reels and search for non-followers. Meta tightened that last filter steadily in 2025–2026 with a teen layer, proactive AI classifiers and originality rules.

### Cited Findings

**AI labels and disclosure**
- Meta labels AI images on Facebook, Instagram and Threads when it detects industry-standard indicators, namely the "AI generated" information in the C2PA and IPTC standards plus invisible markers. It requires people to use its disclosure tool "when they post organic content with a photorealistic video or realistic-sounding audio that was digitally created or altered, and we may apply penalties if they fail to do so." The self-disclosure requirement covers video and audio, not images. A more prominent label may be added for content with "a particularly high risk of materially deceiving the public on a matter of importance." Published 6 Feb 2024, updated 1 Apr 2025 [Meta] — [Labeling AI-Generated Images on Facebook, Instagram and Threads](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/02/labeling-ai-generated-images-on-facebook-instagram-and-threads/)
- Label history [Meta]:
  - 1 Jul 2024: "Made with AI" was renamed "AI info", because industry-signal labels were hitting photos with only minor AI retouching.
  - 12 Sep 2024: content only *edited* with AI tools now carries the label inside the post menu. Content *detected as AI-generated* keeps a visible label that says whether it came from industry signals or from self-disclosure.
  - Labelled content stays up "unless the content otherwise violates our policies." The page ties demotion to fact-checker ratings of "False or Altered", not to the AI label itself.
  - The page was last updated 23 Oct 2025 — [Our Approach to Labeling AI-Generated Content and Manipulated Media](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/04/metas-approach-to-labeling-ai-generated-content-and-manipulated-media/)
- Meta's help page says the label requirement applies to photorealistic video and realistic audio, not to images. Images may still be labelled if detection finds AI generation or modification (via search snippet) [Meta] — [How to identify AI content on Meta products](https://www.meta.com/en-gb/help/artificial-intelligence/1783222608822690/)
- Detection depends on metadata. Metadata can be stripped, fully AI-generated photos can go undetected, and real photos were mislabelled after ordinary Adobe edits [Press] — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2803646); [heise](https://heise.de/-9776887) (pre-2025, possibly outdated)
- Engagement cost of AI labels (not Meta-specific) [Data]:
  - Copenhagen Business School ran experiments with about 700 participants. Labels reduced engagement, most of all on emotional posts: about 46 pp lower emotional engagement for AI-generated emotional content and about 24 pp for AI-enhanced, against about 27 and 13 pp for rational content. Showing the label later softened the hit for AI-*enhanced* content but not for fully AI-generated content — [CBS](https://www.cbs.dk/en/latest/ai-labels-reduce-social-media-engagement)
  - A forthcoming *Journal of Consumer Research* paper (Carney, Riveros, Tully) used more than 1M TikTok posts around TikTok's Sept 2023 disclosure mandate. Disclosure lowered perceived creator effort and authenticity. Coverage reports up to 8% fewer likes and about 7% less overall interaction. More detailed disclosures ("how AI was used") softened the effect — [SSIR](https://ssir.org/articles/entry/ai-disclosures-social-media-disengagement); [Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/ai-labels-can-reduce-social-media-engagement-study-finds/)
- Mosseri's year-end memo (31 Dec 2025) [Press]:
  - "Authenticity is becoming infinitely reproducible." He expects it to be "more practical to fingerprint real media than fake media."
  - Instagram is considering labelling content at the moment of capture and surfacing authentic content.
  - The polished look is giving way to "raw, imperfect" content, and trust will shift from the content to the people who post it.
  - Sources: [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/social-media/instagram-chief-ai-is-so-ubiquitous-it-will-be-more-practical-to-fingerprint-real-media-than-fake-media-202620080.html); [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/instagram-says-ai-killed-the-curated-feed-now-its-scrambling-to-prove-whats-real); [Om Malik](https://om.co/2026/01/01/what-is-instagrams-adam-mosseri-really-saying-in-his-year-end-memo/)
  - Critics note that Meta has admitted it cannot reliably detect AI content — [Om Malik follow-up](https://om.co/2026/01/07/who-decides-whats-real-in-the-age-of-ai-instagram-does/)

**Authenticity, impersonation, inauthentic behavior**
- Inauthentic Behavior policy: Meta does not allow people to misrepresent themselves, use fake accounts, or artificially boost the popularity of content. Networks of inauthentic assets controlled by the same people, used to mislead or evade enforcement, are removed [Meta] — [Inauthentic Behavior](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/community-standards/inauthentic-behavior)
- Account Integrity policy [Meta]:
  - Meta can restrict or disable accounts tied to a previously disabled account. That covers accounts created or repurposed to evade a removal, accounts with common ownership or content, and networks with a clear violating purpose.
  - Continued violations lead to disabling. Meta says disabling is not limited to the strike count and is decided case by case.
  - Sources: [Account Integrity](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/community-standards/account-integrity); [Disabling accounts](https://transparency.meta.com/enforcement/taking-action/disabling-accounts/)
  - In a 2026 Oversight Board matter, Meta disabled an Instagram account under this policy together with other accounts linked to the same user. The Board noted that the restrictions Meta applies before disabling are not public — [Meta case page](https://transparency.meta.com/oversight/oversight-board-cases/account-ban-for-targeting-public-figures/); [Oversight Board PDF](https://www.oversightboard.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/943953711753124.pdf)
- I found no Meta rule that prohibits a fictional AI persona, and none that requires an "AI" label in the bio of an image-only account [Press]:
  - 404 Media wrote that a newer AI-model account did not say it was AI in its bio "despite Instagram's policy requiring it." The specific policy was not identified and I could not verify it.
  - Meta deleted high-profile AI-model accounts that used face-swapped videos of real women after 404 Media contacted it (2024–2025) — [404 Media](https://www.404media.co/ai-influencers-are-deepfaking-their-faces-onto-real-womens-bodies/)

**Sexual solicitation and adult content**
- Implicit or indirect solicitation is removed when content combines two things (via search snippet) [Meta] — [Adult Sexual Solicitation and Sexually Explicit Language](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/community-standards/sexual-solicitation):
  - an implicit offer or ask: "sharing contact information, or suggesting to be contacted directly"
  - a sexually suggestive element: content barred under Adult Nudity, or "regionalized sexual slang, commonly sexualized emojis, sexually suggestive poses, sexual roles, sex positions".
- The Oversight Board reads this as a two-element test: "Content that contains an implicit offer or ask AND Sexually suggestive elements." It overturned removals where the suggestive element was missing — [Columbia Global Freedom of Expression summary](https://globalfreedomofexpression.columbia.edu/cases/oversight-board-case-of-gender-identity-and-nudity) (pre-2025). The Board has also said Meta's internal moderator guidance on sexual solicitation is broader than the public policy, and asked for clearer criteria (via search summary; case attribution not verified) — [Oversight Board](https://oversightboard.com/decision/BUN-IH313ZHJ/)
- Meta "limits viewing of content with usernames, links, or logos of Adult Subscription Websites to adults 18 and older." Links to pornographic websites, and offers of ways to obtain pornographic material, are prohibited (via search snippet) [Meta] — [Adult Sexual Solicitation](https://transparency.meta.com/en-gb/policies/community-standards/sexual-solicitation/)
- Ads with such links or logos are allowed only when targeted to people 18+ [Meta] — [Ad Standards](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/objectionable-content/adult-sexual-solicitation-and-sexually-explicit-language/)
- Since 2019, Meta removes "contextually specific and commonly sexual emojis or emoji strings" when they appear alongside requests for nudes, sex or sex chat [Press] — [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/90424513) (pre-2025, possibly outdated)

**Recommendation eligibility and Account Status**
- The Recommendation Guidelines set a higher bar than the Community Guidelines [Meta] — [Instagram Help](https://help.instagram.com/613868662393739); [Instagram Creators](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators):
  - A sexually suggestive post "will still appear in Feed if you follow the account," but may not appear in Explore or search.
  - Accounts that repeatedly post violating content may be ineligible for recommendation "for a period of time."
  - Professional accounts can check their eligibility in Account Status.
- Since 2019 Instagram has demoted suggestive content without nudity from Explore and hashtags. It described a broader standard to journalists than its help text shows [Press] — [TechCrunch 2019](https://techcrunch.com/?p=1810598); [Quartz](https://qz.com/1594392/instagram-will-demote-inappropriate-content-and-self-expression-along-the-way) (pre-2025, possibly outdated)
- 16 Nov 2026 update [Meta] — [An Update on the Content We Recommend on Instagram in India](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/11/an-update-on-the-content-we-recommend-on-instagram-in-india/):
  - Meta now keeps more "sexually suggestive content and content that depicts violence" out of recommendations in India.
  - It improved detection globally, and now uses more capable AI models to assess content proactively instead of waiting for user reports.
  - People report recommended content about half as often as a year earlier.
- Originality rule (30 Apr 2026) [Press/Meta] — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/instagram-restricts-reach-of-content-aggregators-in-new-crackdown/); [PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2026/04/30/new-instagram-policies-target-reposted-content/); [Instagram Creators](https://creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-originality):
  - Accounts that regularly repost content they didn't create lose recommendation eligibility across the app. This now covers photos and carousels, not just Reels.
  - Instagram judges a rolling 30-day window. Crediting the creator or making superficial edits does not count as original.
  - Eligibility returns once most recent posts are original.
- Account Status (Settings > Account Status) shows removed content, lost features and the recommendation-eligibility line. Practitioners call that line "the only official check" for a suspected shadowban [Vendor] — [Desirely](https://www.desirely.co/en/blog/threads-onlyfans-traffic-agencies); [InstantDM](https://instantdm.com/blog/avoiding-instagram-bans-for-onlyfans-creators-2026-guide)

**Teen-safety layer (2025–2026)**
- 14 Oct 2025 [Meta] — [Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/10/instagram-teen-accounts-pg-13-ratings/):
  - Teens can no longer follow accounts Meta finds "regularly share age-inappropriate content", or accounts whose "name or bio suggests the account is inappropriate for teens."
  - Teens who already follow such accounts can no longer see or interact with them, DM them, or see their comments.
  - These accounts are not recommended to teens and are harder for teens to find in search. Teen recommendations exclude sexually suggestive content.
- TechCrunch gives a bio link to an OnlyFans account as an example of a teen-inappropriate bio [Press] — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/14/instagram-defaults-to-pg-13-content-for-teens-adds-more-parental-controls); [AOL/press headline](https://www.aol.com/news/instagram-big-change-stop-teens-130555168.html). Meta's own post, as fetched, does not name OnlyFans.
- The teen default hides "sexually suggestive poses", among other things [Press] — [PBS NewsHour](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/nation/instagram-says-it-will-limit-teens-to-pg-13-content-by-default-as-safeguard)
- 29 Apr 2026: teens are removed from these accounts' follower lists entirely. Affected accounts are told in Account Status and can delete the non-compliant content [Meta] — [Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/10/instagram-teen-accounts-pg-13-ratings/)

Users blamed AI moderation; Meta did not confirm the cause [Press] — [OECD AI incident](https://oecd.ai/en/incidents/2025-06-16-ec0c); [BGR](https://www.bgr.com/tech/if-you-were-banned-from-instagram-ai-might-be-to-blame/)

### Inferences
- **Compliance posture for the persona:**
  - Switch on Meta's AI disclosure for every photorealistic AI video Reel. It is mandatory and penalties are possible.
  - For stills, disclosure is not mandatory, but saying "AI-generated / virtual" in the bio matches the durable AI influencers in §5 and lowers the risk of exposés and mass reports.
  - Never pair suggestive imagery with contact cues ("DM me", link or "secret" emojis, "see more") in the same post or bio. That is exactly the two-element solicitation test.
- **Reach effect of the label:** the "AI info" label is not documented as a reach penalty, but independent studies point to a modest engagement cost: single-digit percentages in the TikTok field data, larger for emotional content in lab experiments. Mosseri's memo suggests Instagram's long-run direction is to favour verified-real media, so an AI account should expect relative disadvantage over time rather than parity.
- **Lingerie and implied teasers on Instagram/Threads are "non-recommendable" at best.** They reach followers, not new people. Growth content (Reels aimed at non-followers) has to be clean fitness and lifestyle, with suggestive posts kept to a small minority so the whole account doesn't become ineligible.
- **The teen layer creates a new account-level class** ("regularly shares age-inappropriate content" or a teen-inappropriate bio). Landing in it removes all under-18 followers, which is commercially harmless, and Account Status will show it. Whether it also cuts recommendation reach among adults is undocumented.
- **Yoga, pilates and flexibility poses can read as "sexually suggestive poses" to classifiers,** depending on camera angle, framing and clothing (for example, rear-angle splits or low camera angles in tight wear). This is my inference; no source tested yoga content specifically.
- **Compliance and risk by practice** (my assessment from the rules above):

| Practice | Rule status | Ban/restriction risk | Reputation risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Photoreal AI video Reel without the AI disclosure toggle | Violates the disclosure requirement | Medium (penalties unspecified) | High if exposed |
| AI stills, no per-post label, bio says "AI/virtual" | Compliant (image disclosure not mandatory) | Low | Low |
| Stripping C2PA/IPTC metadata from stills | Not prohibited for images | Low | Medium (reads as deceptive if exposed) |
| Hiding AI provenance on video | Non-compliant | Medium | High |
| Suggestive photo plus "DM me" or link emoji | Implicit solicitation, removable | High (strikes, then account-level ineligibility) | Medium |
| Face-swapping the persona onto real women's Reels | Originality, IP and deepfake problems; accounts deleted after press inquiries | High | Very high |

## 2. Linking to Fanvue/OnlyFans from Instagram and Threads

### Takeaway
Meta does not ban adult-subscription links outright. Its policy restricts content with adult-subscription usernames, links or logos to viewers 18+, while porn-site links and explicit or implicit solicitation are removable. In 2025–2026 the risk comes from a link combined with a suggestive profile and solicitation cues. Operators use neutral landing pages, Instagram as a buffer, or Telegram as the hop between the social account and the paysite. They report that the link alone matters less than what surrounds it, but almost all evidence is vendor or anecdotal.

### Cited Findings
- **Policy:**
  - 18+ restriction for adult-subscription usernames, links and logos; porn-site links prohibited (via search snippet) [Meta] — [Adult Sexual Solicitation](https://transparency.meta.com/en-gb/policies/community-standards/sexual-solicitation/)
  - Threads content must follow Instagram's Community Guidelines — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/threads-algorithm/)
- **Teen layer:** accounts with a teen-inappropriate bio (TechCrunch's example: an OnlyFans link) cannot be followed by teens, and since Apr 2026 teens are removed from their follower lists [Meta/Press] — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/14/instagram-defaults-to-pg-13-content-for-teens-adds-more-parental-controls); [Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/10/instagram-teen-accounts-pg-13-ratings/)
- **Threads bio links:**
  - Up to five links in the bio plus link-click insights since 15 May 2025 [Press] — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/05/15/threads-now-lets-creators-add-up-to-5-links-to-profiles-track-clicks)
  - Before that change Mosseri said, "We don't demote links, but we don't place much value on clicks" — [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/social-media/threads-is-finally-embracing-links-150012499.html)
  - In mid-2025 he reportedly said Threads had been ranking links better for over a month (secondary sources) — [Influencer Marketing Hub](https://influencermarketinghub.com/adam-mosseri-links-on-threads-are-performing-better/); [Vista Social](https://vistasocial.com/insights/threads-algorithm/)
- **Operator routing options on Threads** (Sept–Nov 2026) [Vendor/Anecdotal] — [Desirely](https://www.desirely.co/en/blog/threads-onlyfans-traffic-agencies):
  - Three bio options: a direct paysite link (fastest, most exposed), a gateway page (one step of protection, one extra click), or a redirect to Instagram (slower conversion, but the audience is retained).
  - Telegram is "the destination in most cases" and is said to survive ban waves better than other routes. In the measured case "two posts were enough at launch" for the Telegram channel.
  - Use one tracking link per channel, and don't stack several destinations on one redirect page.
  - The author's view: a link alone doesn't trigger sanctions; explicit solicitation and the surrounding profile content do.
- **A second operator funnel** [Anecdotal] — [xcelerator / onlyfanscourse, Mar 2026](https://ofm-course.pages.dev/blog/threads-strategy-ai-ofm-subscriber-growth/):
  - Threads post → Instagram profile with at least 9 posts → link in bio → OnlyFans/Fanvue.
  - Adding monetization links too early cost the author three accounts in one week.
- **Vendor claims about triggers** (May 2026) [Vendor] — [InstantDM](https://instantdm.com/blog/avoiding-instagram-bans-for-onlyfans-creators-2026-guide):
  - Raw OnlyFans links in the bio, DMs or Stories.
  - A Linktree page with an OnlyFans button plus suggestive text can get the main account penalized.
  - Obfuscated spellings ("0nlyF@ns", "L!nk in B!0") are caught by text classifiers.
  - A bikini photo plus peach, water-drop, eggplant or link emoji is flagged as solicitation.
  - OCR reads site logos and watermarks in images and Reels.
  - Sending the same raw link repeatedly in DMs trips spam filters.
  - Suggested set-ups: link-in-bio tools with innocuous button labels; a custom domain pointing to a Carrd/WordPress landing page; or a "clean bio" with a comment keyword that triggers a DM.
- **Vendors disagree on landing pages.** One says a neutral landing page keeps the direct adult link off the platform. Another says a landing page with trigger words, suggestive imagery or adult emojis gets the Instagram account flagged by association [Vendor] — [Inro](https://www.inro.social/blog/avoid-instagram-bans-onlyfans); [unil.ink](https://unil.ink/blog/onlyfans-bio-link-strategy-2026)
- **ManyChat's Acceptable Use Policy** (effective 1 Jul 2026) prohibits violating connected platforms' policies (Meta, Telegram, TikTok), masking true link destinations, and using redirects to evade third-party platform policies — [ManyChat AUP](https://manychat.com/legal/aup)
- **Jan 2025 forum report:** a creator tried several link tools and found Instagram "flagging everything for weeks" [Anecdotal] — [BlackHatWorld](https://www.blackhatworld.com/seo/help-with-link-cloaking.1852410/)
- **Older reports** (pre-2025, possibly outdated): mentioning adult content in the bio, grid or Stories could get an account banned — [Refinery29, 2021](https://refinery29.com/en-gb/2021/01/10275667/instagram-ad-guidelines-onlyfans); Instagram removed link stickers from some adult creators — [Vice](https://broadly.vice.com/en/article/m7vqaa/instagram-is-punishing-users-by-taking-away-their-link-stickers)
- **Context:** TikTok link restrictions are described as a threat to Fanvue's social traffic — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/chat/h/a3c84079-2134-44b4-92c7-265638d67c50/)

### Inferences
- **Linking set-ups compared** (my assessment from the cited rules and reports):

| Linking set-up | Rule status | Ban/restriction risk | Conversion cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Direct Fanvue link in IG or Threads bio | Allowed; restricted to 18+; blocks teen follows | Medium; rises with a suggestive grid, emojis or "DM me" copy | Lowest friction | Keep the rest of the profile SFW |
| Neutral landing page on own domain listing Fanvue | Allowed; Meta can still see the destination | Low–medium, depending on the page's content | +1 click | Keep the page SFW; masking to evade policy breaks tool terms (ManyChat AUP) |
| Link-in-bio aggregator (Linktree, Beacons, AllMyLinks) with an adult button | Same as above | Medium (vendors report flags by association) | +1 click | Use innocuous labels; no explicit thumbnails |
| Telegram channel as the hop | Telegram link is not adult in itself | Lowest on Meta's side | +1–2 clicks, but you own the audience | Teasers live on Telegram, under Telegram's rules |
| Cloaked or rotating redirect links built to evade detection | Against the spirit of Meta policy; explicitly barred by ManyChat AUP | High if detected | — | Avoid |
| Comment keyword → automated DM with link | Allowed if the tool and message comply | Medium (spam filters on identical links) | Vendors claim higher conversion | Vary the copy; no masked redirects |
| Link sticker in Stories | Generally allowed | Medium (adult creators lost stickers before 2025) | Low friction | Keep the Story itself non-suggestive |

- **Most defensible single-account pattern on this evidence:** SFW grid; "virtual / AI-generated" stated in the bio; one neutral hub (own domain or Telegram) that then links to Fanvue; no solicitation copy on Meta surfaces. The persona already has X and Telegram for teasers, which fits this.
- **Threads violations hit Instagram too.** Threads accounts are tied to an Instagram account and bans cascade, so a link problem on Threads can cost the Instagram account.
- **The teen block is a side effect, not a reach penalty.** An adult-subscription link in the bio will probably push the account into the teen-blocked class, which removes under-18s. That only matters if Meta also lowers adult reach for such accounts, and nothing documents that.

### Gaps
- No primary Meta information on whether fanvue.com is classified as an "Adult Subscription Website" or how link classification works.
- No controlled comparison of direct link vs landing page vs Telegram, on either conversion or ban rate. All evidence is vendor or anecdotal.
- Instagram's current number of bio links was not re-verified in this research.

## 3. Instagram growth in 2025–2026 (formats, trial reels, hooks, audio, cadence, Stories/channels/subscriptions, collabs, comments, hashtags, warm-up; fitness niche)

### Takeaway
In 2025–2026, distribution to non-followers is driven by Reels watch time, sends (DM shares) and likes per reach, and originality is now enforced at account level. Hashtags are capped at five and don't add reach. Trial reels let creators test with non-followers. Reels win reach while carousels earn slightly higher engagement. The data puts the "sweet spot" at 3–5 posts a week, with positive but diminishing returns above that. Hooks, audio and collabs rest mostly on vendor evidence.

### Cited Findings
- **Ranking signals:** in January 2025 Mosseri named watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach as the strongest signals, according to marketers' summaries. "Sends per reach" means how many viewers sent the post to a friend by DM. The "3–5× a like" weighting that circulates online is third-party commentary, not Mosseri's [Press/Vendor] — [Buffer 2026 algorithm guide](https://buffer.com/resources//instagram-algorithms/); [Sprout Social](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/instagram-algorithm/); [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/instagram-algorithm-shift-why-sends-matter-more-than-ever/521389/)
- **Hooks:** coverage of Mosseri's posts says he calls the first three seconds of a Reel "the hook" and suggests adding on-screen text (secondhand) — [Inman, Mar 2025](https://www.inman.com/2025/03/07/head-of-instagram-reveals-secrets-to-social-media-success/). Emplifi found that human speech starting within the first 3 seconds, and a person on screen within the first 3 seconds, raise retention. That data is for brand Facebook Reels, not Instagram [Data] — [Emplifi](https://emplifi.io/resources/facebook-reels-data/)
- **Hashtags:** since Dec 2025 Instagram caps hashtags at five per post or Reel, down from 30; some test accounts saw three. Mosseri says hashtags help search but "do not increase your reach" [Press] — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/no-more-hashtag-stuffing-instagram-limits-hashtag-usage-to-just-5-per-reel-and-post-507444-2025-12-19); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-implements-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/); [BetaNews](https://betanews.com/2025/12/19/instagram-puts-a-limit-on-hashtag-usage/)
- **Trial reels:**
  - Launched Dec 2024. They are shown to non-followers first, and the creator sees performance before sharing to followers [Meta] — [Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/12/trial-reels-try-content-non-followers/)
  - Instagram reportedly said in June 2025 that 40% of creators began posting Reels more often and 80% of those saw higher non-follower reach. ## 4. Threads specifically

### Takeaway
OFM operators rate Threads highly for four reasons: its For You feed distributes heavily to non-followers, so reach is only weakly tied to follower count; text-first posts sidestep image classifiers; it has grown to 500M monthly users; and links are no longer de-valued, with five bio links since May 2025. But Threads shares Instagram's Community Guidelines and is tied to an Instagram account, so Threads violations can cascade to Instagram. Meta's own guidance stresses replies and conversation; operators' high-volume tactics are farm-style and risky.

### Cited Findings
- **Scale:** 400M monthly active users in Aug 2025 and 500M in June 2026. Daily users were last reported at 150M in Oct 2025 [Meta/Press] — [Meta, Jun 2026](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/06/meta-launching-new-features-500-million-monthly-threads-users/); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/16/threads-adds-new-personalization-and-community-features-as-it-reaches-500m-monthly-users/)
- **Ranking system** (Meta's explainer updated 7 Mar 2025, as summarized by Buffer in Jan 2026) [Meta] — [Meta Transparency: Threads feed AI system](https://transparency.meta.com/features/explaining-ranking/ig-threads-feed/); [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/threads-algorithm/):
  - The For You feed is ranked by AI; the Following feed is reverse-chronological.
  - The system predicts likes, replies, follows, profile clicks and scroll-past.
  - Inputs include posts seen, recency, the author's like and click rates, Instagram profile views and "predicted guideline-violating language." Weights are not disclosed.
  - Threads says ranking emphasizes timeliness, engagement and topical relevance, and favours active conversations over recycled posts.
- **Replies:** Mosseri has said replying is about as valuable as posting for growth [Press] — [Sendible](https://www.sendible.com/insights/threads-algorithm). Buffer's analysis of 128,000 posts found that replying to comments "can boost engagement by 42%" [Data] — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/threads-comments-engagement/)
- **Tags:** Threads allows one topic tag per post (per coverage of Instagram's hashtag cap) [Press] — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/no-more-hashtag-stuffing-instagram-limits-hashtag-usage-to-just-5-per-reel-and-post-507444-2025-12-19)
- **User feed controls:** "Dear Algo" (Feb 2026) works through a public post and lasts up to about 3 days. "Your Algo" (June 2026) does the same privately, in the US, Canada, UK, Australia and New Zealand [Press] — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/11/threads-new-dear-algo-ai-feature-lets-you-personalize-your-feed/); [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/threads-500-million-users-your-algo-communities)
- **Links:** see §2 (five bio links, link insights, not demoted).
- **API caps:** 250 API-published posts and 1,000 API-published replies per profile per rolling 24 hours [Meta] — [Meta for Developers: Threads overview](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/threads/overview); [Threads replies docs](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/threads/retrieve-and-manage-replies)
- **Rules:** Threads content follows Instagram's Community Guidelines, and a Threads account requires an Instagram profile. Buffer infers that content excluded by Instagram's Recommendation Guidelines, such as sexual content, likely doesn't reach For You [Press] — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/threads-algorithm/)
- **Why operators like Threads** [Vendor/Anecdotal] — [Desirely](https://www.desirely.co/en/blog/threads-onlyfans-traffic-agencies):
  - Reach depends on staying recommendation-eligible; follower count matters little. Operators report 1,000-follower accounts pulling 50,000 views a day while a 50,000-follower account pulled 20,000.
  - Text-only posts avoid image classifiers. Suggestive photos are demoted, not deleted.
  - The platform is "saturated with model accounts, many AI-generated," so distinct positioning is needed.
  - The virality window is "often 48 hours", and fixed content calendars don't work.
  - A drop from 20,000 to 4,000 views in one day is a warning sign.
- **A second operator's playbook** [Anecdotal] — [onlyfanscourse](https://ofm-course.pages.dev/blog/threads-strategy-ai-ofm-subscriber-growth/):
  - Text-forward posts with AI photos; hooks built on relationship status, age framing and vulnerability; winning images reposted under new captions.
  - Several original posts plus several comments a day, spread across sessions.
  - "Comment leeching": commenting on posts with 2,500+ likes so the profile picture draws clicks.
  - The author claims Threads posts reach older, higher-spending audiences through Facebook and Instagram cross-distribution.
  - My summarizer flagged that the guide includes age-framed sexual caption categories, a child-safety red flag.
- **Warm-up and cadence, then and now** (farm context) [Vendor/Anecdotal]:
  - February 2025 playbooks used slow warm-up: 10–20 likes and 5–10 comments, ramping from 1 to 2 to 3 posts over five days.
  - July 2026 playbooks use no warm-up: link in bio immediately, 35–40 posts on day one and every day after, described as "six to seven times under the technical ceiling" — [Desirely](https://www.desirely.co/en/blog/threads-onlyfans-traffic-agencies)
  - A different operator lists early links, bulk or rapid actions, shared Wi-Fi, obviously AI imagery and purchased aged accounts as ban triggers — [onlyfanscourse](https://ofm-course.pages.dev/blog/threads-strategy-ai-ofm-subscriber-growth/)
- **Ban triggers observed** [Anecdotal] — [Desirely](https://www.desirely.co/en/blog/threads-onlyfans-traffic-agencies):
  - Machine-like regularity: many accounts posting at the same minute on the same pattern.
  - Meta restricted one operator's third-party scheduler overnight; the appeal was still pending months later.
  - Spring 2026 waves hit accounts with 100k+ followers.
  - Burning Threads accounts burns the linked Instagram accounts.
- **Unverified volume claim:** an agency video claims 30–50+ subscribers a day from Threads while upselling a course and bot [Anecdotal] — [SozAI transcript](https://sozai.app/transcript/full-threads-marketing-guide-onlyfans-2026-ofm/)

### Inferences
- **For a single persona account (not a farm),** the evidence supports:
  - 1–3 text-first posts a day: questions, opinions, relatable "40+, fitness, expat life in Dubai" prompts, with occasional SFW photos.
  - Active, manual replies under her own posts and in relevant large threads. Mosseri and the Buffer data both point to replies.
  - One topic tag per post.
  - Bio links (up to five) to a neutral hub.
- **Sexualized captions probably cost reach even when they aren't removed.** Meta lists "predicted guideline-violating language" as an input to follow and profile-click predictions (inference from the signal list).
- **Keep age framing on the maturity side.** "40 and…" hooks are core to this niche and fine. Anything youth-coded ("girl", school, teen terms) or "age play" framing should be avoided entirely, because child-safety enforcement is Meta's most aggressive category (inference).
- **35–40 posts a day is a disposable-account tactic.** On a main brand account it looks like spam (machine-like regularity) and puts the linked Instagram account at risk.
- **Compliance and risk by tactic** (my assessment):

| Tactic | Complies? | Ban risk | Reputation risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Text plus question posts, occasional SFW photo | Yes | Low | Low |
| Mild engagement prompts ("reply with your…") | No explicit Threads engagement-bait rule found; Meta says it favours "value" | Low–medium | Low |
| Manual, relevant replies on big posts | Yes | Low | Low |
| Mass identical replies / scaled "comment leeching" | Spam / inauthentic behavior | High | Medium |
| Suggestive photos | Allowed if non-explicit; demoted from For You | Medium | Low |
| 35–40 scheduled posts a day | Within the API cap but spam-like; schedulers have been restricted | High | Low |
| Cross-posting Instagram content to Threads | Yes | Low | Low |
| Bio link to Fanvue or a hub | Allowed with the 18+ restriction | Medium | Low |

### Gaps
- No Meta primary statement on engagement bait on Threads.
- No independent data on Threads → Fanvue conversion.
- No Threads-specific policy for adult creators beyond the shared guidelines.
- Not independently verified: whether every new Threads sign-up still requires an Instagram account in late 2026. Buffer (Jan 2026) and Desirely (Nov 2026) both say it does.
- Not verified: the operator claim that Threads posts reach older, higher-spending audiences through cross-distribution.

## 5. Case studies of AI influencers on Instagram

### Takeaway
There are two models. Brand-deal virtual influencers (Lil Miquela, Lu do Magalu, the Instagram side of Aitana López) disclose in the bio and earn from sponsorships. Adult-funnel AI personas (Aitana's Fanvue, Sika Moon, many anonymous accounts) use Instagram as the top of the funnel and monetize on Fanvue. Fast growth of 150k–300k followers within 5–8 months is documented, but revenue figures are mostly self-reported. Accounts that hid AI status or stole real women's bodies are the ones that drew exposés and deletions.

## 6. Benchmarks: follower growth and profile visit → link click → paid subscriber

### Takeaway
No authoritative benchmark exists. Practitioner best-case targets are about 3% of impressions → profile visits, 5–10% of profile visits → link clicks, and 4–8% of link clicks → paid subscribers. Vendors put overall conversion at 0.5–3% of profile visitors subscribing, with DM funnels claimed at 5–8%. Normal follower growth at 3–5 posts a week is about +0.26% a week (Buffer median). Viral AI accounts are outliers.

### Cited Findings
- **Follower growth:**
  - Buffer medians, from +0.12% at 1–2 posts a week to +0.66% at 10+ (see §3) [Data] — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/library/how-often-to-post-on-instagram/)
  - AI personas: Aitana reached about 280k in roughly eight months (2023) — [OMR](https://omr.com/en/daily/ai-models-fanvue). Mia Zelu reached about 169k in roughly five months (2025) — [UTS](https://www.uts.edu.au/news/2025/08/monetising-a-mirage)
  - On Threads, views are weakly tied to followers: 1k followers pulled 50k views a day versus 20k for a 50k-follower account [Anecdotal] — [Desirely](https://www.desirely.co/en/blog/threads-onlyfans-traffic-agencies)
- **Funnel targets from an AI-OFM operator** [Anecdotal] — [onlyfanscourse, Mar 2026](https://ofm-course.pages.dev/blog/threads-strategy-ai-ofm-subscriber-growth/):
  - 100+ likes per winning post.
  - 3%+ profile visits from impressions; 5–10% link clicks from profile visits; 4–8% subscriptions from link clicks.
  - $20–40 revenue per subscriber in the first month, and 50%+ retention at 30 days.

## 7. Multi-account ("farm") strategies and Meta's enforcement (observed practice and its risks, not a recommendation)

### Takeaway
In 2025–2026, OFM operators run batches of disposable Threads/Instagram accounts, dozens per model, each with its own antidetect browser profile, dedicated proxy and SIM, and they accept high attrition. This directly violates Meta's Inauthentic Behavior and Account Integrity rules. Meta's 2026 AI-driven fake-account purges, its linked-account disabling and its originality rules raise both the attrition and the risk of collateral bans on any main brand account that is linked to the farm.

### Cited Findings
- **Observed farm economics** [Vendor/Anecdotal] — [Desirely](https://www.desirely.co/en/blog/threads-onlyfans-traffic-agencies):
  - 40–60 accounts launched to keep 20 alive, with about a dozen active at any moment.
  - Losing about 4 a week from a batch of 20 takes about 5 launches a week just to hold steady.
  - The channel pays "from roughly ten live accounts up."
  - One antidetect browser profile, user agent and dedicated proxy per account; a US IP and US SIM for US targeting.
  - Randomized posting times, because simultaneous posting degrades reach.
  - "Watch accounts" fed with likes to spot trending formats.
  - Threads bans burn the linked Instagram accounts.
- **Another operator** manages 37 creators across 450+ pages, recommends 2–3 backup accounts per operation with each VA running 2–3 accounts, and lists buying aged accounts and shared Wi-Fi as ban triggers [Anecdotal] — [onlyfanscourse](https://ofm-course.pages.dev/blog/threads-strategy-ai-ofm-subscriber-growth/)
- **A dissenting practitioner:** a 2025 creator video summary warns against traffic methods that depend on proxy or account farms [Anecdotal] — [YouTube summary](https://youtubesummary.com/summary/2Nbz2PRvJJo)
- **Meta's rules** [Meta]:
  - Inauthentic Behavior bans fake accounts, misrepresentation and artificially boosted popularity; networks controlled by the same people are removed — [Inauthentic Behavior](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/community-standards/inauthentic-behavior)
  - Account Integrity disables linked and evasion accounts, case by case beyond strikes — [Account Integrity](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/community-standards/account-integrity)
  - Legitimate multi-account use: switching between up to five accounts per device is supported — [Instagram Help](https://help.instagram.com/iphone-app/1696686240613595)
- **Enforcement:**
  - The May 2026 AI-driven fake-account removal caused mass suspensions and follower losses — [OECD](https://oecd.ai/en/incidents/2026-05-08-e34b)
  - Linked accounts were disabled together in a 2026 Oversight Board matter — [Meta case page](https://transparency.meta.com/oversight/oversight-board-cases/account-ban-for-targeting-public-figures/)
  - The Digital Rights Foundation told the Board that repeat offenders evade bans with new accounts or VPNs, while wrongly targeted users lack meaningful appeal — [DRF submission, 2026](https://www.oversightboard.com/wp-content/uploads/gravity_forms/80-95790c13f80f72ed73275bba35ddf55d/2026/02/DRF-Submission_-Board-to-Review-for-First-Time-Meta-Approach-to-Disabling-Accounts.pdf)
  - Originality rules penalize accounts that recycle others' content (Apr 2026) — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/instagram-restricts-reach-of-content-aggregators-in-new-crackdown/)
  - Meta restricted an operator's scheduler, and spring 2026 ban waves hit operators [Anecdotal] — [Desirely](https://www.desirely.co/en/blog/threads-onlyfans-traffic-agencies)
  - Audiences built on fake engagement can cost monetization eligibility — [Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/instagram-partner-monetization-policies/)
- **Reputation:** "AI pimping" exposés covered networks of AI accounts funnelling to paysites [Press] — [404 Media](https://www.404media.co/inside-the-booming-ai-pimping-industry-3/); [Marketplace](https://www.marketplace.org/episode/ai-pimping-accounts-are-exploding-on-social-media)

### Inferences
- **Farms are non-compliant by design.** Ban risk is high, and the damage can spread to any linked main account through shared devices, IPs, phone numbers, payment methods or the same Fanvue link. This persona's value lies in one consistent identity across five platforms, so the main Instagram and Threads accounts are the asset. Any farm-like activity, including buying aged accounts, engagement networks or synchronized schedulers, puts them at risk.
- **The permitted version is small and under one owner.** For example, a main account plus a backup or fan page managed via account switching. Using extra accounts to evade enforcement is not permitted.
- **Farm economics are getting worse.** The 2026 enforcement trend (proactive AI classifiers, linked-account disabling, originality rules, removal of teen followers) is moving against them, and practitioners' own reported attrition (~20% of a batch per week) reflects that.

### Gaps
- I found no Meta-published 2025–2026 numbers for fake accounts removed specifically on Instagram or Threads.
- Practitioner attrition rates and revenue claims are unverified.
- No documented case of a main brand account being banned because of a farm linked to it. The cascade risk is inferred from Meta's Account Integrity policy and from operators' reports of IG/Threads bans spreading.
