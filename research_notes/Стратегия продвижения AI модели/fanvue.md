# Fanvue as the monetization hub for a photoreal AI persona (2025–2026)

Source labels used below: [OFFICIAL] = Fanvue help center / legal site / Fanvue blog; [ANALYST] = Sacra; [PRESS] = news outlets; [3P] = third-party blog, vendor or agency site (often affiliate-linked, medium-to-low confidence); [OPERATOR] = self-reported operator or course/referral seller (low confidence, unverified screenshots); "(pre-2025)" = possibly outdated. Compliance tags: COMPLIANT / GREY / VIOLATION. Research date: 2026-11-21. Reddit could not be fetched by the research tools, so practitioner evidence comes from operator blogs and press, not Reddit threads.

## 1. Fanvue's rules for AI creators: disclosure/labeling, verification, prohibited content, enforcement

### Takeaway
Fanvue explicitly welcomes fully-AI accounts but frames them tightly. The human operator must pass KYC, and every AI account carries a platform AI label. All AI media needs a clear disclosure (bio, caption, watermark or accompanying message), and a fully-AI persona must never tell fans it is a real person. AI characters must look clearly adult; stated ages and prompts don't count. No real person's likeness, and no AI content built from other people's photos, without that person's verified consent. Penalties scale from content removal and Discover removal to payout holds, earnings deduction and permanent ban. The official help pages were refreshed in Aug–Oct 2026, so these rules are current.

### Cited Findings
**Account types and verification**
- Fanvue defines three AI account types. (1) "Fully AI": a virtual character whose photos, video and voice are AI-generated and don't depict a real person; the owner needs their own KYC. (2) "AI persona based on a real person" (deepfake/face swap): KYC plus verification and signed consent of that person. (3) "AI-enhanced creator": a real person using AI for some content. "All three types must show the AI label." [OFFICIAL, page dated Oct 16, 2026] — [Fanvue Help: AI content on Fanvue: what's allowed](https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/9538738-is-ai-content-allowed-on-fanvue)
- Every creator account, AI accounts included, is verified ("required by law"). The account owner completes ID verification with a government ID and a selfie even if their face never appears in content, and the ID is not shown on the profile. Fully AI characters don't need co-creator documents. [OFFICIAL, updated Oct 16, 2026] — [Fanvue Help: Verifying your AI account and anyone you feature](https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/9539091-verifying-your-ai-account-and-anyone-you-feature)
- One verified identity can run up to 15 AI accounts and 2 human accounts. [OFFICIAL] — [Verifying your AI account](https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/9539091-verifying-your-ai-account-and-anyone-you-feature)
- An account registered as a real (human) account whose content shows someone other than the verified holder cannot be fixed with co-creator documents, because "fans are paying for and talking to the verified person". [OFFICIAL] — [Verifying your AI account](https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/9539091-verifying-your-ai-account-and-anyone-you-feature)
- The platform tag observed on AI profiles reads "AI generated or enhanced". [3P, re-verified Oct 5, 2026] — [VerifiedHer: Fanvue AI creators roster](https://verifiedher.com/fanvue-ai)

**Disclosure**
- Community Guidelines Section D: AI media needs a "clear, prominent disclosure, such as a watermark, caption, accompanying message, or bio statement". It must not be misleading or harmful, and must not impersonate real people other than the account owner. [OFFICIAL, undated] — [Fanvue Community Guidelines](https://legal.fanvue.com/community-guidelines)
- The help center says disclosure can go in the profile bio, the caption or a watermark, and that failing to disclose may lead to removal and/or suspension. It also states: "If your account is fully AI, never tell fans you're a real person." [OFFICIAL] — [Help: AI content](https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/9538738-is-ai-content-allowed-on-fanvue)
- Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) 2.3.4 requires AI content to be "Properly disclosed if generated using AI or synthetic tools". AUP 2.6.2.6 prohibits "AI-generated imagery without clear disclosure" in profile pictures, banners, intro videos and public bios. [OFFICIAL, undated] — [Fanvue Acceptable Use Policy](https://legal.fanvue.com/acceptable-use-policy)
- Unverified vendor claims: that disclosure must appear in every paid DM, and that creators declare an "AI Creator" designation at signup. Neither appears in the official text reviewed. [3P] — [fanvy.ai blog](https://fanvy.ai/blog/disclose-ai-fanvue-without-killing-conversion)

**Age and appearance**
- "Prompt text or disclaimers are not sufficient to overcome visual resemblance to individuals under 18." [OFFICIAL] — [Community Guidelines §D](https://legal.fanvue.com/community-guidelines). The help center says the same: "Prompts, captions, and stated ages don't override how the content looks." — [Help: AI content](https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/9538738-is-ai-content-allowed-on-fanvue)
- AI media must pass a "Reasonable Person's Test" run by three or more moderation team members. A failed test means removal, and multiple failures can mean permanent suspension. [OFFICIAL] — [Help: AI content](https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/9538738-is-ai-content-allowed-on-fanvue)
- Public media must not use age-baiting language; examples given are "barely legal" and "teen" (AUP 2.6.2.5). Age-play is banned (Guidelines C.6.3). [OFFICIAL] — [AUP](https://legal.fanvue.com/acceptable-use-policy); [Community Guidelines](https://legal.fanvue.com/community-guidelines)

**Real people and source material**
- No AI content that looks like an identifiable real person, celebrities and other creators included, unless that person is verified and has given documented consent. Fanvue states: "An AI-altered face is not proof of consent." No AI content made from other people's photos or videos. [OFFICIAL] — [Help: AI content](https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/9538738-is-ai-content-allowed-on-fanvue); [Verifying your AI account](https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/9539091-verifying-your-ai-account-and-anyone-you-feature)
- Internal inconsistency: the same help article embeds an older "AI-generated media policy", dated "October 3rd" with no year, which says AI media "must not depict a real person other than the account owner". That conflicts with the consent-based route described above. [OFFICIAL] — [Help: AI content](https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/9538738-is-ai-content-allowed-on-fanvue)
- Also banned for AI characters: offensive imitation of disabilities or illnesses, and copyrighted characters. [OFFICIAL] — [Help: AI content](https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/9538738-is-ai-content-allowed-on-fanvue)
- The API policy separately bans nudify tools and deepfake or face-swap tools that create intimate imagery of a real person without documented consent and identity verification. [OFFICIAL] — [Fanvue API Access & Usage Policy](https://legal.fanvue.com/api-policy)

**Prohibited categories relevant to fitness and flexibility content**
- Guidelines C.6.3 bans (non-exhaustive list): bestiality, choking/asphyxiation, sadomasochistic abuse, hardcore bondage, bloodplay, bodily fluids, and incest including "step" scenarios. [OFFICIAL] — [Community Guidelines](https://legal.fanvue.com/community-guidelines)
- Oct 12, 2026: sexual content involving family members is now prohibited, "including fictional or role-play scenarios". Dangerous acts or challenges that could encourage imitation are also banned, with an exception for activities done with appropriate expertise and safety measures. [OFFICIAL] — [Fanvue legal changelog](https://legal.fanvue.com/changelog)
- Sept 25, 2026: the AUP now bars requesting, arranging, offering or promoting in-person meetings with users. [OFFICIAL] — [Changelog](https://legal.fanvue.com/changelog)
- Masks, face coverings and cultural or religious attire are allowed in AI media only if the content is disclosed and doesn't mislead viewers into thinking it shows a real person. [OFFICIAL] — [AUP 2.5.2](https://legal.fanvue.com/acceptable-use-policy); [Guidelines §D](https://legal.fanvue.com/community-guidelines)

**Public media and Discover rules (Guidelines Section F, AUP 2.6)**
- Profile picture, banner and intro video must be SFW: no nudity, implied nudity, see-through lingerie or explicit posing. Opaque swimwear, lingerie or boudoir is allowed if it would suit mainstream social media. Intro videos must feature only the creator, be non-sexual, have no explicit language in the first seconds, and use no fetish outfits or props. [OFFICIAL] — [Community Guidelines §F](https://legal.fanvue.com/community-guidelines)
- Penalties: first offence, the profile is removed from Discover until fixed; later offences, the profile becomes inaccessible to the public. "This does not delete your content, ban your account, or affect your earnings from existing subscribers." [OFFICIAL] — [AUP 2.6.4](https://legal.fanvue.com/acceptable-use-policy)

**Enforcement**
- Sanctions include a warning, content removal, payouts held during review, deduction of earnings from that content, suspension, and account closure. Content can be removed without notice. [OFFICIAL] — [Help: AI content](https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/9538738-is-ai-content-allowed-on-fanvue)
- After a breach Fanvue may "Revoke Creator Balance or User Balance in part or in full" and "may prevent withdrawals" (AUP 7.1.3). Permanent bans (7.1.5) and law-enforcement referral (7.1.4) are also possible. [OFFICIAL] — [AUP](https://legal.fanvue.com/acceptable-use-policy)
- Account statuses are restricted, suspended or banned. Listed triggers include "AI content that doesn't follow our rules on age or real people". Opening a new account after a suspension or ban is not allowed and will be closed, and earnings may be deducted or forfeited. [OFFICIAL, via search summary] — [Help: Why is my account banned/suspended?](https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/8046997-why-is-my-account-banned-suspended)
- Aug 11, 2026: the moderation appeal window was extended from 7 to 14 days, and people depicted in content can appeal consent decisions even if they are not Fanvue users. [OFFICIAL] — [Changelog](https://legal.fanvue.com/changelog)
- Fanvue told Business Insider it uses a third-party moderation tool plus human moderators, and that "deepfakes are an industry challenge". [PRESS, Nov 2024, pre-2025] — [Business Insider via AOL](https://www.aol.com/influencers-using-ai-women-lead-093001152.html)
- Observed disappearances of AI accounts: Emily Pellegrini's Fanvue page returned a missing-page error by July 9, 2026, and Emily Hart's page also died. The cause can't be determined from outside, since deletion, disabling and renaming look the same. [3P] — [VerifiedHer](https://verifiedher.com/fanvue-ai)
- Operator anecdote: one of 8 AI models got a temporary ban "during a policy tightening wave", which the operator blamed on aggressive PPV scripting by a new chatter. The operator says Fanvue enforces more strictly than OnlyFans. [OPERATOR, Apr 2026, referral-linked] — [AI Influencers Launch: Fanvue review](https://aiinfluencerslaunch.com/fanvue-review)

### Inferences
- **Age:** for a 40-year-old persona the under-18 rule is low-risk, and the visible maturity works in the persona's favour for compliance. The residual risk comes from inconsistent generations. Keep age cues consistent (face, skin, styling). Avoid younger-looking variants, petite or childlike styling, school-type props, and words like "girl", "teen", "young" or "barely legal" in public media. Three moderators judge appearance, and a stated age of 40 won't save an image that reads younger.
- **Face origin:** the face must be original, not trained on or swapped from an identifiable real person's photos. Otherwise the account falls into the "AI persona based on a real person" category and needs that person's KYC and signed release. Accidental resemblance to a celebrity or another creator is also a ban trigger.
- **Disclosure:** belt-and-braces disclosure costs little. Use an AI statement in the bio, keep the platform AI label, and add a small caption or watermark on sets. The backstory (Dubai, yoga/pilates trainer, age 40) is fine as character fiction, provided the account never claims to be a real human.
- **Flexibility content:** stay clear of choking, bondage, fluids and "step"/family role-play. Present extreme contortion as trained practice rather than a "challenge" for fans to copy, given the Oct 2026 dangerous-acts rule.
- **Religious attire:** Dubai-themed religious or cultural attire (e.g., abaya or hijab) is technically allowed if disclosed. Sexualizing it carries high reputational risk; recommend avoiding it.

## 2. Monetization mechanics: subscriptions, PPV, messages, tips, bundles, trials, tracking, referrals, fees, payouts, DM vs subscription share

### Takeaway
Fanvue has the full OnlyFans-style toolkit plus some extras: paid or free subscriptions, PPV posts and messages, tips, multi-month bundles, targeted promos, free-trial and tracking links, segmented mass messaging, automations, a 5% creator-referral program and a creator-funded affiliate program. The standard fee is 20%. Fanvue publishes no split of revenue by source, but says the largest source of message-driven revenue is ordinary 1:1 conversation. The best audited benchmark is OnlyFans FY2025, where 73% of revenue came from messages, PPV and tips versus 27% from subscriptions.

### Cited Findings
**Fees and payouts**
- Standard fee is 20%: "Fanvue charges low platform fees: 20% of what you earn." [OFFICIAL, blog Jan 5, 2026] — [Fanvue blog: What is Fanvue? (2026)](https://www.fanvue.com/blog/what-is-fanvue). Sacra also says 20% applies across subscriptions, tips, one-time payments and paywalled content. [ANALYST] — [Sacra: Fanvue](https://sacra.com/c/fanvue/)
- Third-party reports of a new-creator 85/15 promotion conflict on its length: the first 30 days, a window after KYC, or 12 months. One review says the homepage advertised "85% for the first month, then 80%" in July 2026. [3P, unverified] — [ofm-tools review](https://ofm-tools.com/fanvue-review/); [exclu.at fees](https://exclu.at/blog/fanvue-fees-payouts); [fanvuemodels payout methods](https://fanvuemodels.com/blog/fanvue-payout-methods). A third-party site citing a help article updated May 4, 2026 says the legacy 85% rate for 2022 joiners has ended. [3P] — [exclu.at: What is Fanvue](https://exclu.at/blog/what-is-fanvue)
- Payouts: "Fanvue also offers instant payouts." [OFFICIAL] — [Fanvue blog](https://www.fanvue.com/blog/what-is-fanvue). Third parties report a 1% fee only on crypto withdrawals (TripleA), no Fanvue fee on bank transfers, and a minimum around $20. [3P] — [tryclout fees](https://www.tryclout.ai/blog/fanvue-fees); [exclu.at fees](https://exclu.at/blog/fanvue-fees-payouts)
- One operator reports a first payout after 9 days and crypto withdrawals landing in 24–48 hours. The same operator claims a $50 fee per dispute once the chargeback rate exceeds 1.5% over a rolling 30 days. [OPERATOR, Apr 2026] — [AI Influencers Launch](https://aiinfluencerslaunch.com/fanvue-review)
- Fan payment methods: Visa and Mastercard (credit, debit and prepaid), Apple Pay, Google Pay, PIX (Brazil) and a crypto wallet. [OFFICIAL, via search summary] — [Help: Payment methods](https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/7860539-payment-methods-on-fanvue)

**Product toolkit**
- Subscription "tiers" from $3.99 to $100/month, and following can be free if the creator allows it. Paywalled posts cost $3–$500. Also available: pay-to-view messages, tips, bundled content, checkout links, and automated messages triggered by follows, renewals or first messages. [OFFICIAL, Jan 2026] — [Fanvue blog](https://www.fanvue.com/blog/what-is-fanvue)
- Promotional offers are a free trial or a discounted first month, targetable to All, New, Expired or Followers. Subscription bundles give up to 50% off for 3, 6 or 12 months paid upfront. Free-trial links are unique links for specific campaigns or funnels. [OFFICIAL, updated Aug 10, 2026] — [Help: Running Profitable Promotions](https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/16237907-running-profitable-promotions)
- Free-trial links live under Settings → Creator Settings → Subscription → "Create new free trial link", where you set the number of uses, expiry and trial length. This is separate from the "Follow for Free" toggle (Settings → Profile). [OFFICIAL, via search summary] — [Help: How do I create a free trial link?](https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/7860789-how-do-i-create-a-free-trial-link)
- Tracking links are unlimited and show which promotions drive traffic and conversions. [OFFICIAL, via search summary] — [Help: Settings full overview](https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/7860532-settings-full-overview)
- Default messaging lists: Subscribers, Followers, Free Trial Subscribers, Expired/Non-Renewing, "Spent more than $50" (called "a fairly low bar") and Auto-Renewing. [OFFICIAL, updated Aug 10, 2026] — [Help: Mass Messaging: Driving Revenue Without Spamming](https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/16237736-mass-messaging-driving-revenue-without-spamming)
- Checkout Links launched July 23, 2026 with Buyer/Seller terms. Since Nov 16, 2026, adult, sexually explicit and NSFW content and services, including AI companion and adult chat services, are prohibited on Fanvue Checkout. Adult content stays allowed on the main platform. [OFFICIAL] — [Changelog](https://legal.fanvue.com/changelog)
- Rewards "Points" launched Sept 17, 2026: no cash value, non-transferable, claim within 48 h, held 7 days, expire after 30 days if unspent. [OFFICIAL] — [Changelog](https://legal.fanvue.com/changelog)

**Retention by promo type (Fanvue's own data)**
- Bundles do best: about 1 in 8 churn in the first month, about 4 in 10 are still subscribed after 90+ days, and these fans spend about 3.5x more after their first month than fans who joined free. [OFFICIAL] — [Help: Running Profitable Promotions](https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/16237907-running-profitable-promotions)
- Free trials and trial links: about 1 in 4 stay past 90 days, and they spend about 5x less over their lifetime than paying subscribers. [OFFICIAL] — same
- Time-limited discounts do worst: about 7 in 10 leave within the first month, and about 1 in 5 stay past 90 days. Fanvue calls bundles "the healthiest way to offer a lower price" and warns that constant discounting teaches fans to wait for deals. [OFFICIAL] — same

**Referral and affiliate programs**
- Creator referral pays 5% of each referred creator's earnings for their first 12 months. You cannot refer your own second account. [OFFICIAL, dated Oct 6, 2026] — [Help: Referrals](https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/9526966-referrals-everything-you-need-to-know)
- Sept 19, 2026: "Qualifying Revenue" was defined. Attribution windows are 36 months for referred creators registered before 9 April 2026 and 12 months for later registrants, and commission on Checkout Link purchases is calculated on net proceeds. [OFFICIAL] — [Changelog](https://legal.fanvue.com/changelog)
- Affiliates: any creator can open an offer paying 5–50% commission to people who promote them, and affiliates keep earning while those fans renew. [OFFICIAL, via search summary of help article] — [Help: Affiliates](https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/16238105-affiliates-earn-commission-by-promoting-other-creators). Sacra adds a 50% cap, up to five promoted creators per affiliate, and a $50k commission cap per promoted creator; these were not confirmed in Fanvue's own text. [ANALYST] — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/fanvue/)

**Where revenue comes from**
- Fanvue: "the largest source of message-driven revenue is ordinary 1:1 conversation, not broadcasts". The New Subscriber welcome message is "most-used and among the highest earning". - OnlyFans benchmark (Fenix International FY2025 accounts, year to Nov 30, 2025, filed Aug 2026): messages, PPV and tips brought 73% of OnlyFans' revenue and subscriptions 27%. Messages/PPV/tips grew from $943M to $1.13B (+20%), while subscriptions fell 10%, from $470M to $422M. The top 100 fans (0.01%) accounted for 20.2% of spending, at $1,397–$59,030 each. [3P agency data] — [OnlyTraffic](https://onlytraffic.com/blog/onlyfans-statistics-and-analytics)
- AI-creator guide: "20% of subscribers generate 80%+ of revenue" and PPV is the primary source (unsourced). [3P, June 2026, affiliate-linked] — [Baltic Assets guide](https://balticassets.eu/en/guides/how-to-monetize-ai-model-fanvue-2026/)

### Inferences
- Treat the subscription as the gate and DMs/PPV as the revenue engine. By analogy with OnlyFans (73% non-subscription) and Fanvue's own statement about 1:1 chat, a mature account should expect the majority of revenue from messages, PPV and tips, concentrated in a few high spenders. This makes chat capacity and quality the main scaling constraint.
- For discounts, follow Fanvue's own data: 3- and 6-month bundles at 20–50% off beat time-limited discounts. Use free-trial links only as tracked, capped campaigns (limited uses and expiry), e.g., a per-channel link for X or Telegram, because trial fans retain worse and spend about 5x less.
- Give each traffic source and campaign its own tracking link (Instagram bio, Threads, X pinned post, Telegram channel posts) so traffic quality can be compared. This is fully compliant.
- Don't sell NSFW sets through Fanvue Checkout Links (e.g., directly from Telegram). Since Nov 16, 2026 that is a violation. Route all adult sales through the main Fanvue profile.
- The creator-referral program (5% for 12 months) is negligible for this use case. The affiliate program, where you pay others 5–50%, is a compliant way to buy promotion from other creators or promoters.

### Gaps
- No Fanvue-specific public split of earnings by source (subscriptions vs PPV posts vs messages vs tips), overall or for AI creators.
- The current new-creator fee promotion couldn't be confirmed on Fanvue's own pages; check it in the dashboard.
- The chargeback-fee threshold is an operator claim only.
- The affiliate help article was seen only via search summary and was not fetched in full.

## 3. Pricing benchmarks: subscriptions, PPV ladders, launch promos, AI vs real, mature niche

### Takeaway
Fanvue allows $3.99–$100/month subscriptions and $3–$500 priced posts. AI-operator guides cluster around a $5–15/month entry subscription and $5–20 PPV items, with higher tiers at roughly $20+. Fanvue's own data favours multi-month bundles over discounts. No reliable dataset compares AI with real creator pricing, and claims about mature/MILF niche economics come only from creator-marketing blogs.

### Cited Findings
- Allowed ranges: subscriptions $3.99–$100/month; paywalled posts $3–$500. [OFFICIAL, Jan 2026] — [Fanvue blog](https://www.fanvue.com/blog/what-is-fanvue)
- Concrete AI-persona example: an Instagram AI influencer's Fanvue subscription cost $7/month. [PRESS, Nov 2024, pre-2025] — [Business Insider via AOL](https://www.aol.com/influencers-using-ai-women-lead-093001152.html)
- AI-creator guide: core subscription $7–15/month, a $4.99 teaser tier, a $19.99 upsell tier, and PPV at $5–20 for exclusive photo sets or videos. Suggested scarcity promos include "First 10 subs get 30% off", 24-hour flash sales and "50% off for first 20 subs". It estimates 90–120 days to $1,000/month and calls 80–85% monthly retention "the norm" (unsourced). [3P, June 8, 2026, affiliate-linked; arithmetic errors noted on page] — [Baltic Assets guide](https://balticassets.eu/en/guides/how-to-monetize-ai-model-fanvue-2026/)
- An operator running 8 AI models advises starting subscriptions at $4.99–$9.99. [OPERATOR, Apr 2026, referral-linked] — [AI Influencers Launch](https://aiinfluencerslaunch.com/fanvue-review)

## 5. DM/chatting: operator practices, funnels/scripts, Fanvue rules on automation and AI disclosure in chat

### Takeaway
Fanvue permits, and itself provides, AI-assisted and autonomous chat. Three rules govern it: the creator is responsible for every message, a fully-AI persona must never claim to be a real person, and a fan who sincerely asks whether they're talking to AI must get an honest answer (binding on third-party apps through the API policy). Operators typically combine an AI first response with human chatters who close PPV sales. Fanvue's own playbook: welcome-message automations, segmented lists, 1:1 selling to high spenders, and restraint on broadcasts.

### Cited Findings
**Rules**
- "You're responsible for everything sent from your account, including messages written by AI. Check drafts before you send them." If a fan asks directly whether they are talking to AI, answer honestly; proactive disclosure in each chat is not required by this article. [OFFICIAL, updated Oct 19, 2026] — [Help: AI tools on Fanvue](https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/9539500-ai-messaging)
- "If your account is fully AI, never tell fans you're a real person." AI content must follow the Community Guidelines, including in messages. [OFFICIAL] — [Help: AI content](https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/9538738-is-ai-content-allowed-on-fanvue)
- API policy (rule wording from a search summary of the policy page; the AI-disclosure section itself is confirmed by the changelog): apps that generate or send messages with AI, or run an AI persona or chatbot, must act only with the creator's authorization, must let the creator pause or switch off AI messaging at any time, and must not deny being AI when a fan sincerely asks. Since Oct 16, 2026 the API policy applies to all apps (listed or not), requires OAuth, and has an AI-disclosure section covering AI media, AI chat and personas, real-person likeness and minors. [OFFICIAL] — [API policy](https://legal.fanvue.com/api-policy); [Changelog](https://legal.fanvue.com/changelog)
- The AUP bans automated programs, bots and crawlers used to access Fanvue or harvest content (5.6), limits request volume to what "a human can reasonably produce" in a standard browser (5.3), and bans falsely depicting "live interactions" (2.3.3). Content must be "Authentically produced by the Creator or their approved collaborators" under the Co-Authored Content Policy (2.3.1.1). [OFFICIAL] — [AUP](https://legal.fanvue.com/acceptable-use-policy)
- Contrast: OnlyFans' terms prohibit models from using AI chatbots and allow AI content only when it features the verified creator. That effectively excludes fully-AI personas, which is why they concentrate on Fanvue. [PRESS, Nov 2024, pre-2025] — [Business Insider via AOL](https://www.aol.com/influencers-using-ai-women-lead-093001152.html)

**Fanvue's own funnel and script guidance**
- Set up the New Subscriber welcome first ("most-used and among the highest earning"). Use the New Follower welcome to build the relationship and promote subscribing, not to hard-sell PPV. Send a renewal thank-you. The cancellation win-back earns little, but a teasing image may help. [OFFICIAL] — [Help: Mass Messaging](https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/16237736-mass-messaging-driving-revenue-without-spamming)
- Broadcast only when there is something worth saying (a drop, offer or milestone), point subscribers to new PPV as soon as it's live, and use broadcasts for tip prompts. Avoid messaging fans you haven't connected with, hard-selling PPV to followers, mass-messaging top spenders, and over-messaging. [OFFICIAL] — same
- The welcome message can carry attached images or video with an unlock price. [OFFICIAL] — [Help: Top 6 ways](https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/7860525-top-6-ways-to-get-your-first-fan-on-fanvue)

**Operator practice (anecdotal)**
- An agency with 8 AI models on Fanvue uses Fanvue's native AI messaging on every model for first responses, while human chatters close PPV sales; chatter training is "critical". One model received a temporary ban, blamed on a new chatter's aggressive PPV scripting. [OPERATOR, Apr 2026, referral-linked] — [AI Influencers Launch](https://aiinfluencerslaunch.com/fanvue-review)
- Emily Pellegrini's anonymous creator initially worked 14–16 hour days building the model and later spent about 8 hours a day interacting with fans. [PRESS, Feb 2024, pre-2025; seen in a search summary of these two sources, and which one carries the quote was not confirmed] — [Fortune](https://fortune.com/europe/2024/02/21/ai-influencers-secretive-creators-thousands-dollars); [NetInfluencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/ai-influencers/)

- Third-party chat tools (e.g., FanWake) connect through Fanvue's official API and hold replies for creator approval. [3P vendor] — [FanWake](https://fanwake.app/)
- A guide recommends a 24-hour reply standard for DMs and a weekly rhythm of teaser or PPV drops several times a week plus a Sunday DM catch-up. [3P] — [Baltic Assets guide](https://balticassets.eu/en/guides/how-to-monetize-ai-model-fanvue-2026/)

### Inferences
- A compliant chat stack would look like this. AI First Message Reply plus a priced welcome PPV for new subscribers. AI drafts (Reply/Generation) reviewed by a human or a small chatter team who follow a written persona bible covering voice, backstory and boundaries. A hard rule in every script and bot prompt: if a fan sincerely asks "are you real / AI?", confirm the persona is AI-generated, in a warm in-character way. Never claim "I'm real", never stage "live" proof, and never discuss meeting in person.
- In-character fiction (e.g., post-class stories from a Dubai studio) appears acceptable, since Fanvue's own chatbot chats in a "Creator Personality". The line is crossed by asserting the persona is a real human or by falsifying live interaction.
- Sell to high spenders 1:1 and keep them out of mass messages. Use segmented broadcasts for drops. Avoid pressure tactics (fake scarcity pushed hard, guilt scripts). They raised enforcement risk in the one documented case and plausibly drive chargebacks.
- Prefer Fanvue-native or OAuth-API tools. Browser-automation "auto-chatters" that drive the web UI violate AUP 5.3/5.6 and risk the whole account, including the balance, which can be revoked.

### Gaps
- No official Fanvue position on hiring human chatters or agencies, or on whether chatters must be disclosed.
- No verified data on chat conversion rates (subscriber to first PPV) or on the revenue impact of AI vs human chatting on Fanvue.
- Reddit, BlackHatWorld and Discord practitioner threads couldn't be accessed by the tools.
